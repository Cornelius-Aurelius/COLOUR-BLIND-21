HOW TO PLAY

Two skills. One unforgettable game.

1. Blackjack 21. Get nearer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Hit for another card, stand to stay, or double your stake for one final card. Card ranks are standard and the dealer stops at 17.

2. Scout the shirt. After each hand, compare the target shirt with three choices. Use its number, badge and pattern — colour isn't enough. Correct answers add virtual points, and hints are optional.

WHY THE GAME EXISTS

More than a football game.

Colour Blindness 21 — Matchday Masters is an original educational web game conceived and created by Cornelius Aurelius. It uses football kits to show why sports communication should not rely on colour alone.

For practical accessibility guidance, see Colour Blind Awareness's FA/UEFA resources. This site is independently created and not affiliated with the FA or UEFA.
